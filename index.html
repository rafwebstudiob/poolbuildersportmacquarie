Building a pool in Port Macquarie

Port Macquarie's warm climate gives a long swimming season. Coastal suburbs such as Flynns Beach, Tacking Point and Lighthouse Beach can have sandy soils, salt air and, on the headlands, sloping blocks that change excavation and crane access.

Low-lying streets near the Hastings River, Settlement Point or Lake Cathie may be covered by flood or coastal mapping, and bushland edges can be mapped as bush fire prone. A good builder will check the council planning certificate before quoting, along with how a compliant barrier will work with existing fences.

Do I need approval for a new pool?

Usually, yes. NSW pools need council or certifier approval (often complying development), a licensed builder, a principal certifier, and an occupation certificate before the pool is used. See our NSW pool approvals guide.

Do I have to register the pool?

Yes. All pools and spas in NSW must be registered on the NSW Swimming Pool Register.

Do you cover Flynns Beach, Lighthouse Beach, Lake Cathie, Sovereign Hills, Bonny Hills, Tacking Point, Thrumster and Settlement Point?

What's the difference between concrete, fibreglass and plunge pools?

Concrete is built on site and can be custom shaped. Fibreglass is a pre-formed shell that is delivered and installed. A plunge pool is a smaller pool in either material. See our pool types guide.
